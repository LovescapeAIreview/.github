## Lovescape AI

A place to create and chat with AI companions in the browser. Pick a ready character or build your own. Talk, share images, and hear voice replies on paid plans. Realistic and anime styles are both available.

### What you can do

**Browse characters**  
Look through ready-made companions with different looks and personalities. Some are more mature in style. Open one and start a conversation.

**Create your own**  
Set the name, appearance, personality and background. Save the character and return to the same chat later.

**Chat and media**  
Text conversation is the base. On paid plans you can generate pictures of the character and receive voice messages. Short clips are available on higher tiers.

**Stories and scenes**  
You can guide the conversation into different moods and situations. The character stays in the role you set.
